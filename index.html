How We Work

From First Chat to Finished Work

Every project follows the same clear steps, so you always know what happens next, what it costs and when you will get your files. Scroll down to walk through them.

  1. 1

    Chat & Quote

    Tell us about your business and what you need, by WhatsApp, phone or email. We reply with a clear quote that shows what is included and how long it will take.

    Reply within 1 to 2 working days
  2. 2

    Approve & Pay a Deposit

    Accept the quote and pay a 50% deposit by bank transfer. This books your project, and work starts once the deposit is received.

    50% before work starts
  3. 3

    First Ideas

    We share two to three first ideas for you to look at, such as logo directions or a first cut of your video, with the colours and style we suggest.

    About 3 to 5 working days
  4. 4

    Feedback & Changes

    Tell us what you like and what to change. Two rounds of changes are included, and extra rounds are quoted separately. Replying within 2 working days keeps your project on schedule.

    2 rounds of changes included
  5. 5

    Final Payment & Delivery

    When you approve the work, you pay the remaining 50%. Then we send all your final files, ready for print and online, with simple guidelines on how to use them.

    Files sent after final payment
  6. 6

    Support After Delivery

    For two weeks after delivery, we answer your questions and make small adjustments. New work is quoted separately.

    2 weeks of support

Typical timings

  • Logo designAbout 7 to 10 working days
  • Full brand identityAbout 3 to 5 weeks
  • Social media & print designsAbout 2 to 5 working days
  • Video & animationDepends on length, agreed in your quote

Timings start once your deposit and project details are received, and your quote confirms the exact dates. Prices are in AED, with VAT added where it applies.
