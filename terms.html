Terms of Work

A simple guide to how we work together, so everyone knows what to expect. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

01Design Services

These terms apply to logo, branding, social media, print, video and animation work done by Little Nzou Creative Studio, a registered UAE business. Every project starts with a written quote that lists what is included, the price in AED and the expected timeline. Accepting the quote and paying the deposit means you agree to these terms.

02Payments

  • Deposit: 50% of the quote is paid by bank transfer before work starts. This books your project in our schedule.
  • Final payment: the remaining 50% is paid when you approve the work. Final files are sent once it is received.
  • Small jobs: for small items such as a single social media post or a business card, we may ask for full payment up front.
  • Prices are in AED. VAT is added where it applies and shown on your invoice.
  • Deposit refunds: the deposit covers the time reserved for you and the first ideas, so it is non-refundable once work has started. If we have not yet started, we will refund it.

03Changes and Feedback

Two rounds of changes are included in every quote. Extra rounds, or changes that go beyond the original brief, are quoted separately before we start them. Replying within 2 working days helps keep your project on schedule.

04Timelines

Timelines in your quote start once your deposit and project details are received. If feedback or materials are delayed on your side, dates move accordingly. If a project is paused for a long time, we may need to rebook it into our schedule.

05Ownership and Use

  • Once the final payment is received, the final approved design is yours to use for your business.
  • Until the final payment is received, ideas and draft files remain with Little Nzou Creative Studio and may not be used.
  • We may show finished work in our portfolio and on social media. If you prefer that we do not, tell us before the project starts.
  • Fonts, stock images and music from other creators keep their own licences. We will tell you if any of these need to be bought separately.

06Cancelling a Project

You can cancel at any time by messaging us. The deposit stays with us to cover work completed up to that point. If more work has been done than the deposit covers, we will tell you the difference before anything further is charged.

07Design Packs from Our Online Shop

This section covers downloadable products from shop.littlenzou.com, such as brand kits, logo templates, stationery and print templates.

  • No refunds after purchase. Design packs are digital files that you can download straight away and cannot be returned, so all sales are final once you have bought them.
  • Please check before you buy. Read the product description and preview images, and message us if you have any questions. We are happy to help before you pay.
  • If something is wrong, such as a file that will not open, a missing file or a product that is not as described, message us within 7 days and we will fix it, replace it or refund you.
  • Licence: you may use a pack for your own business or your own clients' projects. You may not resell, share or re-upload the original files.
  • Free downloads are provided as they are, with the same licence rules.
Hardware and equipment bought from the shop, such as gaming items and gadgets, follows the shop's own policy shown at checkout.
