Case Study · Full Brand Identity

Breakfast Basket Brand Kit

A complete visual identity system for a Zimbabwean breakfast-foods house: logo suite, colour and type standards, and a packaging design language carried across coffee, honey, granola and oats.

Prepared by Little Nzou Creative StudioBrand Identity & PackagingDubai, UAE

01

Brand Overview

A pantry staple, rebuilt

Breakfast Basket (ZW) is a Zimbabwean breakfast-foods house producing coffee, honey, granola and oats for local and regional shelves. The brand needed one identity system that could hold together across a genuinely varied product range: a bold single-origin coffee, a delicate honey jar, and a family of cereal packaging, without splintering into unrelated designs per product.

We built a master brand system anchored on a wheat-and-ribbon mark, a fixed three-colour palette, and two paired typefaces, then applied it as a flexible packaging language: a striking export-facing black-and-gold coffee line under its own sub-brand name, "Inzovhu," alongside cleaner, illustration-led packaging for the honey, granola and oats range.
